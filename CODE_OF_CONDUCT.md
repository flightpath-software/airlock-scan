# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and project maintainers are committed to supporting 
a community where participation is respectful, professional, and free from 
harassment to the extent reasonably within our control. We do not tolerate 
harassment based on age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, 
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, 
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, 
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards 
of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may take corrective action, including 
moderation or access restrictions, in response to behavior they reasonably 
determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, harmful, or inconsistent 
with this Code of Conduct.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions. We may 
explain moderation decisions where doing so is consistent with protecting 
privacy and the integrity of the review process.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address,
posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed
representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers responsible for enforcement at
**conduct@flightpath.fm**.

Reports are reviewed by one or more maintainers. Maintainers may gather additional 
context, speak privately with those involved, or consult with other maintainers 
before making a decision. Not every report will result in enforcement action.

The amount of review performed will depend on the nature of the report, the 
information available, and the potential impact on the community. Decisions may 
be based on publicly available information, information voluntarily provided 
by those involved, or information provided directly to the maintainers. We may 
be unable to take action where there is insufficient information.

We will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of those involved. 
Information may be shared among maintainers when necessary to evaluate or respond 
to a report.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The maintainers of this project are responsible for interpreting and enforcing 
this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: Possible actions include a private, written warning, providing 
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the 
behavior was inappropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence**: Possible actions include a warning with consequences for 
continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including 
unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a 
specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community 
spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these 
terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: Possible actions include a temporary suspension of participation
with the community for a specified period of time. Violating these terms may 
lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: Possible actions include a permanent ban from any sort of 
public interaction within the community.

## Appealing a Decision
If you believe an enforcement decision was based on a factual misunderstanding, you may 
reply to the original communication with any additional information you believe is relevant.

For permanent bans, you may request a secondary review by emailing **[conduct@flightpath.fm](mailto:conduct@flightpath.fm)** 
with the subject line `Appeal: <decision-summary>`. Where reasonably possible, a maintainer 
who was not the primary decision-maker will review the appeal and determine whether the 
original decision should be upheld, modified, or reversed.

Appeals are limited to correcting factual misunderstandings, identifying relevant information 
that was not previously considered, or raising concerns about the enforcement process. 
Decisions after appeal are final.

## No Guarantee of Outcome

This Code of Conduct is intended to guide participation in this project. It does not create a 
contractual obligation, employment relationship, legal duty, or guarantee of any particular 
process, outcome, or response time.
